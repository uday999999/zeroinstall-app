1. Fundamental Privacy Guarantee: 100% On-Device Processing

ZeroPro (developed by ZeroInstall) is designed with a strict zero-data-collection philosophy. We believe that security software should protect your privacy, not compromise it. All threat detection, file inspections, notification scans, and security policies execute strictly on your device locally in memory.

We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or rent your personal information, messages, contacts, location, photos, or browsing history to any third parties or remote servers.

2. Explicit Android Permission Disclosures

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies, below are clear and prominent disclosures regarding each privileged permission requested by ZeroPro:

A. BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE (APK Install Blocker)

Purpose: ZeroPro uses the Android AccessibilityService API exclusively to detect when an unauthorized APK installer interface (e.g., PackageInstaller or unknown package source prompts) is opened on the screen.

Limitation: The service does NOT observe user keystrokes, personal messages, passwords, or financial transactions. No screen content is ever saved or transmitted off the device.

B. BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE (Message Link Guard)

Purpose: ZeroPro scans URLs in incoming notifications (such as SMS, WhatsApp, and Telegram) to check whether they contain malicious .apk download links, drive-by malware payloads, or known phishing domains.

Limitation: Links are verified transiently in local RAM. Message texts are never stored, logged, or forwarded anywhere.

C. VpnService (Local Tracker & Spyware Firewall)

Purpose: ZeroPro creates a local on-device VPN loopback to inspect outbound domain queries and block known spyware, stalkerware, and invasive telemetry tracking servers.

Limitation: No external VPN server is used. Traffic is NOT redirected to external servers, and user internet traffic is never analyzed or logged.

D. BIND_DEVICE_ADMIN (Tamper & Policy Guard)

Purpose: Used solely to prevent malware or unauthorized third parties from secretly uninstalling ZeroPro or bypassing "Install Unknown Apps" security policies.

Limitation: Users can deactivate this permission at any time directly through the app or Android device settings.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

ZeroPro includes an optional rewarded ad extension mechanism for users who choose to unlock Pro days for free. If you choose to watch an ad, standard anonymized advertising identifiers may be processed by certified ad networks (such as Google AdMob) strictly to serve and verify the ad playback. Paid subscribers experience zero ads.
