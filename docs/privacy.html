ZeroPro ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") is engineered around a strict 100% On-Device Privacy Architecture. We do not operate external tracking databases, and we do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or monetize your personal data.

1. Explicit Android Permission Disclosures (Google Play Compliant)

To provide proactive endpoint security against unauthorized APK malware and malicious links, ZeroPro requests specific privileged Android permissions. Each permission is strictly executed locally in on-device memory:

A. Accessibility Service API (BIND_ACCESSIBILITY_SERVICE)

Specific Purpose: Used solely to detect when the system package installer window (e.g. com.google.android.packageinstaller or com.android.packageinstaller) is opened to sideload an unknown .apk file.

Strict Privacy Boundaries:

  • The Accessibility service does NOT log, read, or capture user keystrokes, personal messages, passwords, or banking credentials.
  • No screen text or user interactions are recorded, saved to disk, or transmitted off the device.
  • Users can enable or disable this feature at any time in Android Accessibility Settings.
B. Notification Listener Service (BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE)

Specific Purpose: Scans incoming notification messages (such as WhatsApp, Telegram, and SMS) solely to inspect embedded URLs for known phishing domains, bank fraud links, or hidden .apk malware downloads.

Strict Privacy Boundaries:

  • URL evaluation occurs transiently in volatile RAM.
  • Message bodies, chat histories, sender contact details, and OTPs are never stored, logged, or uploaded to any cloud or remote server.
C. Query All Packages (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES)

Specific Purpose: Required strictly on-device to inspect installed applications, uncover invisible background apps without launcher icons (ghost apps), identify sideloaded packages, and audit which apps hold high-risk permissions (Camera, Microphone, Location).

Strict Privacy Boundaries: Your list of installed applications is processed 100% locally on your device and is never shared, sold, or transmitted to any server.

D. VPN Service (BIND_VPN_SERVICE)

Specific Purpose: Creates a local, on-device loopback interface to act as a local DNS sinkhole firewall, blocking outbound connections to known spyware, telemetry loggers, and ad trackers.

Strict Privacy Boundaries: No remote VPN proxy server is used. Your web traffic is never redirected through any external network, analyzed, or stored.

E. Device Administrator (BIND_DEVICE_ADMIN)

Specific Purpose: Used solely upon explicit user activation to protect ZeroPro against unauthorized tampering or uninstallation by stealth malware and to enforce system policies blocking unverified APK sources.

Strict Privacy Boundaries: This permission does not access personal data and can be revoked by the user at any time in Settings.
